# Loop: The Timeline Journaling App Where Your Past Self Comes Back

**Log your day in order. Loops bring your thoughts back — even as full-screen alarms.**

"Two years ago today, what was I doing?" If that question takes more than three seconds to answer, this post is for you.

[Loop](https://devnoyo0123.github.io/) is a **local-first journaling app** built around one idea: your past self comes back to visit you. You write a log, set a *loop*, and at the moment you chose — a gentle notification or a full-screen alarm — that log returns to your timeline with an empty continuation waiting right underneath. Reply, and the chain grows.

## No folders. Just a timeline.

No categories, no tabs, no endless organizing. Loop is **one chronological stream**. When you continue an old log, the reply grows right where the original sits on the timeline — so context is never lost.

If you've ever quit a journaling habit because "organizing" ate all the energy, this design is for you.

## Loops: reminders that come back as logs

A *loop* is a log scheduled to return:

- **Notification or full-screen alarm** — your choice, per loop. The alarm keeps ringing until dismissed.
- **Repeat rules** — every N minutes/hours, daily, or weekdays (Korean public holidays are skipped automatically).
- **Missed fires are caught up** — phone was off? Entries come back in their original time slots, in order.
- **Forgetting-curve review** — each time a loop comes back, the suggested interval grows: 10 min → 1 hour → 1 day → 1 week → 1 month.

## More than text

- **Photos, place & weather** — attach from the gallery or shoot in-app; location and weather are captured per log (coordinates stay on-device).
- **Full-text search** — SQLite FTS5 trigram index with BM25 relevance sorting, including substring search. Years of logs, found in a second.
- **Flow stats** — current & best streak, a 15-week heatmap, "your thoughts came back N times", and your longest chains.
- **"Then, you"** — every morning at 9, a log from a week, a month, or a year ago today comes back to visit (optional).

![Continuing an old log — a reply grows right where the original entry sits on the timeline](https://devnoyo0123.github.io/assets/shots/en/04_flow.webp)

## Your data stays yours — and survives phone switches

Everything is stored **on-device** (local-first; the only network call is a weather lookup). Export any chain as a single `.looplog` file, share it to a nearby device, or rely on Android auto backup and device-to-device transfer. Merge-import by unique IDs never duplicates.

## FAQ

**Is Loop free?** Yes — free on Google Play.

**Does my journal leave my phone?** No. Logs live on-device; you can export everything as a `.looplog` file whenever you want.

**Which languages are supported?** English, 한국어, 日本語, 中文, Español, Tiếng Việt, Italiano, हिन्दी — 8 languages.
